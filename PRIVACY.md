# Bokumi privacy

**Short version:** Bokumi has no ads, no analytics and no account. Your library stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync, and then it goes to your own private iCloud, not to us.

### What stays on your device
Your library, categories, reading progress, bookmarks, history, downloads, the files you import, backups and settings. Passwords and tokens for trackers and servers are stored in the iOS Keychain and are never included in backups or sync.

### When Bokumi talks to the internet
Only when you use something that needs it:
- **Extensions and servers you add** contact the websites or servers they were made for, and only the addresses they showed you before you installed them. Bokumi does not add your account information to those requests, and extensions never see your iCloud, sync data or tracker logins.
- **Trackers** (AniList, MyAnimeList, MangaUpdates, Kitsu) are contacted only after you log in, to update your reading lists.
- **iCloud sync** (Bokumi Plus) keeps your library, progress and settings in the private database of your own iCloud account. We can't see it.
- **Apple** handles purchases, subscriptions and iCloud under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

### What Bokumi does not do
No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporters that phone home, no accounts, and no selling or sharing of data.

### Deleting your data
Deleting Bokumi removes everything on the device. If you used sync, remove Bokumi's data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
