## ✦ The short answers

<details><summary><b>What is Bokumi?</b></summary><br>A cozy manga and comics reader for iPhone and iPad: a shelf, a calm series page, five reading modes, themes, widgets and sync. It is a reader app, like a web browser for your books: it hosts nothing and comes with no manga, comics or sources of its own.</details>

<details><summary><b>How do I get my books in?</b></summary><br>Import CBZ, ZIP, EPUB files or whole folders from the Files app, read from a server you run at home, or add a source you set up yourself. The <a href="https://bokumi.app/guides/your-books/">bring your own books</a> guide shows every way.</details>

<details><summary><b>What can I read in it?</b></summary><br>What you own or have the right to read: books you bought without copy protection, your own work, the public domain, and what creators share freely. Bokumi does not condone piracy, and our <a href="https://bokumi.app/terms/">terms</a> forbid it.</details>

<details><summary><b>Is Bokumi free?</b></summary><br>Reading is free, and so is bringing your books in. Bokumi Plus adds themes, widgets, sync, downloads, tracking, automatic backups and more. All sales are final.</details>

<details><summary><b>Which devices does it run on?</b></summary><br>iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon through “Designed for iPad”. Liquid Glass shows up on iOS 26. Android: coming soon.</details>

<details><summary><b>When can I get Bokumi?</b></summary><br>Soon. Bokumi is coming to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and to Android. Neither is available yet, and there are no downloads anywhere else in the meantime.</details>

<details><summary><b>Does Bokumi collect my data?</b></summary><br>No ads, no analytics, no account. Your shelf lives on your device, and sync goes through your own private iCloud. See <a href="https://bokumi.app/privacy/">the privacy policy</a>.</details>
