## ✦ FAQ

<details><summary><b>When can I get Bokumi?</b></summary><br>Soon. Bokumi is coming to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and to Android. Neither is available yet.</details>

<details><summary><b>Is Bokumi free?</b></summary><br>Reading is free, with no ads: your library, history and updates, every reading mode, importing your own books, light and dark mode and app lock. Bokumi Plus unlocks the rest: the other themes and app icons, widgets, offline downloads, iCloud sync, trackers, automatic backups and extra reader options. Plus unlocks app features, never content. If Plus lapses, nothing is deleted. All sales are final; refunds are handled by Apple.</details>

<details><summary><b>Does Bokumi come with manga?</b></summary><br>No. Bokumi is a reader. Bring what you own or have the right to read: your own CBZ, ZIP and EPUB files, a media server you run at home, or a source you set up yourself. Bokumi does not condone piracy.</details>

<details><summary><b>Can I bring my library from another reader app?</b></summary><br>Yes. Make a backup in the other app, then restore it in Bokumi under More → Backup &amp; restore. Your series, progress, categories, history and trackers come along, and restoring never deletes anything.</details>

<details><summary><b>What does Bokumi know about me?</b></summary><br>Nothing leaves your device unless you turn something on: iCloud sync goes to your own private iCloud, and a tracker only hears from Bokumi after you log in to it. There is no analytics and no account. See <a href="PRIVACY.md">PRIVACY.md</a>.</details>

<details><summary><b>Are extensions safe?</b></summary><br>Each extension runs in its own sandbox. Before you install one, Bokumi shows which web addresses it may contact, and it can't reach anything else. Extensions never see your iCloud, your sync data or your tracker logins. Still, only add extensions you trust.</details>
