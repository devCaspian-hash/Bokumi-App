# Bokumi privacy

**Short version:** Bokumi has no ads and no analytics. Your library stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.

### What stays on your device
Your library, categories, reading progress, history, downloads, backups and settings. Passwords and tokens for trackers and servers are stored in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and are never included in backups.

### When Bokumi talks to the internet
Only when you use something that needs it:
- **Extensions and servers you add** contact the websites or servers they were made for. Bokumi does not add your account information to those requests.
- **Trackers** (AniList, MyAnimeList, MangaUpdates, Kitsu, Shikimori, Bangumi) are contacted only after you log in, to update your reading lists.
- **Sync** sends your library and progress to a sync server **you** choose, and only if you sign in to one.

### What Bokumi does not do
No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporters that phone home, no selling or sharing of data.

### Deleting your data
Uninstalling Bokumi removes everything on the device. If you used sync, you can delete your account and all synced data from More → Sync.
